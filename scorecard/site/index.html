Not counted yet

Every figure is a floor: users paid at least this much. These costs are real and not yet included:

  • Slippage and pool fees: the price moving against a trade, and the trading pool's own fee.
  • Front-running: losses to bots that trade just before and after a user.
  • Fees paid in tokens other than SOL, and referral cuts paid out later.
  • Tips to landing services that don't publish their accounts, including seven of Axiom's eight tip accounts.

How it works

  • Each app collects its fee into public wallets. Every successful transaction that paid one of them is a trade on that app.
  • For each trade, the trader is the wallet that signed it and swapped a token. Its SOL change, minus refundable account deposits, gives the trade size.
  • Costs are the SOL that landed in the app's fee wallets, the network fee the trader paid, and SOL sent to published tip accounts.
  • This edition samples each app's most recent trades. The code is open and anyone can re-run it.
