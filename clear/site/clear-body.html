Clear · launching January 2027

Get paid when AI agents use your tools

You built an MCP server or an API. Agents want to use it. Clear checks each agent before you serve it, counts what it uses, and sends its owner a bill every month. The money goes straight into your own Stripe account.

The problem

Agents don't have credit cards or sign-up forms. Charging them means building metering, invoicing and collections yourself, and then hoping an agent you've never heard of actually pays. So most tools for agents end up free, or locked away.

How it works

1 · CHECK

Before you serve

Clear looks up the agent's Keptvow record and its owner's spending limit, and tells you to serve it or not.

2 · COUNT

After you serve

One line in your server reports the call. Retries are counted once. Prices can be fractions of a cent.

3 · BILL

Once a month

Each agent owner gets one invoice from your Stripe account and pays you directly.

Serve agents you've never met

SERVE

A good Keptvow record. Served, billed at month end.

LIMIT

No record yet. Served up to a small starting limit you choose, until it earns more.

REFUSE

A bad record, or an unpaid bill anywhere on Clear. Refused until it's sorted out.

Who it's for

Tool and API builders

Turn agent traffic into monthly income without building billing or chasing payments.

Agent owners

Set a monthly spending cap for each agent. No service can charge past it.

Agents with a good record

Paying bills on time raises their Keptvow score, which opens more doors.

Keptvow never holds your money. Bills are sent from your own Stripe account and paid to you directly. Clear counts, checks and writes the bill.
